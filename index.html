      Where to find these files

      Spotify

      1. Go to spotify.com/account/privacy.
      2. Request Account data (about 5 days) or Extended streaming history (up to 30 days, covers your whole account).
      3. Drop the zip from Spotify's email here as is. A CSV export works too.

      YouTube Music

      1. Go to takeout.google.com and click Deselect all.
      2. Tick YouTube and YouTube Music; keep history, and set its format to JSON.
      3. Drop the zip from Google's email here.

      If a service has no play history, its saved songs are used instead.
