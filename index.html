      Where do I get these files?

      Spotify

      1. Go to spotify.com/account/privacy.
      2. Tick Account data (arrives in about 5 days) or Extended streaming history (up to 30 days, covers your whole account).
      3. Download the zip from Spotify's email and drop it here as is.

      YouTube Music

      1. Go to takeout.google.com and click Deselect all.
      2. Tick YouTube and YouTube Music. Under All YouTube data included, keep history (and music-library if you like).
      3. Under Multiple formats, set history to JSON (HTML works too, just slower).
      4. Download the zip from Google's email and drop it here.

      If a service has no play history, its saved songs are used instead.
